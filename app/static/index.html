How do I analyze a YouTube or Twitch recording?

Use YouTube Studio or Twitch Video Producer to download an original recording you own, then open Videos → Upload a recording. The platform APIs provide official channel data but do not grant Clip Studio access to a downloadable video file.

Monitor an authorized HLS livestream (disabled on free test)

Free Render instances go to sleep and have temporary storage. Live monitoring is intentionally disabled for this test build.

Provide a direct HTTPS .m3u8 feed that you control or have been authorized to capture. This is not a Twitch/YouTube URL importer or OAuth connection.

Each completed approximately two-minute segment produces new review candidates while the feed is live. Recording starts only when you click Start. Stream connections stop on server restart; this initial build does not auto-resume.
